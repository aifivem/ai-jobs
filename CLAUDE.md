# ai-jobs

Work routes started at a depot: driving routes (taxi, bus, trucker, garbage, news) with a work vehicle and pay per stop, towing (a broken-down car on the flatbed to the depot), and gathering routes on foot (recycle, vineyard, diving) that give items per stop. One engine, every route and stop set in the panel. Next to it the hot dog stand (port of qb-hotdogjob), its own mode. Depends on ai-core (jobs, money, prompts, progress); gives work vehicle keys through ai-core's key layer (lib `vehicleKeys`) and uses the active inventory through ai-core's inventory layer (lib `inventory`).

## Decisions

- One combined engine instead of a resource per job: Qbox's job scripts are the same loop (go to a point, do a timed action, get paid). A route's `kind` picks the order: `random` (any other stop next), `ordered` (stops in id order, wraps), `gather` (on foot, items instead of money), `tow` (a locked car spawned at the stop; loading it makes the depot the next stop, paid there).
- The server picks every stop and keeps the shift in memory (lib `PlayerMap`, vehicles deleted on drop); the client only shows the stop it was sent. Nothing is stored in the DB: a shift ends on leave or resource stop. A player who drops while the work vehicle or the towed car spawns gets nothing stored and the vehicles are deleted (`isOnline` after every await).
- A stop's finish is refused when the player is more than 12 m away, sooner than the way from the last stop (or the depot) at 60 m/s plus 80% of the stop time (teleports), or (driving routes) more than 15 m from the work vehicle. A tow is paid only when the towed car is within 15 m of the flatbed at the depot (`JOBS_ERR_TOW_LOST`).
- Per route in the panel: gather item count range (`gatherMin` / `gatherMax`), the tow route's car models (`towModels`), the depot blip (`blip`, default sprite by kind). Towed cars spawn locked through `spawnVehicle({ locked: true })`.
- Stops are their own panel records naming their route, so each one gets a `point` widget; a route without stops cannot start. The server keeps them per route in id order through `RecordObjects` (one stop edit touches its route's list); routes are `panel.records` on the server and `RecordObjects` (depot points) on the client.
- Who may work a route or rent the hot dog stand: the lib access fields (`jobs`, `gangs`, `onDuty`; empty = anyone), checked with `online.allows` on start and `allows` in the depot's / counter's `canShow`. A locked route's depot and blip show only to who passes; memberships rebuild only the locked routes.
- Pay goes to the bank (`work:<route>` ledger reason).
- Hot dog stand: not a route (no stops, no timed stop action, the stand is pushed anywhere and sells to passers-by), so a separate mode in the same resource: panel settings file `hotdog` (`sync: "all"`, one counter as QB; defaults are qb-hotdogjob's spot, `prop_hotdogstand_01`, $250 bank deposit, cash sales, levels at rep 0 / 50 / 100 / 200 with stock 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 and its price ranges; the stand heading is QB's spawn `w - 90`; access fields empty by default like the routes, QB requires the `hotdog` job). Flow: rent at the counter (deposit back when given back there, lost on logout, refunded on a resource stop), the stand's point opens ai-lib's menu (push, prepare, selling on / off), let go with the `jobs_hotdog_release` keybind (G) or by falling, dying, aiming, fighting or entering a vehicle.
- The stand is no networked object: the server keeps each stand in a lib `AreaStream` (followers start on character load, `followAll` every 2 s while stands exist) and every client draws a local copy; while pushed, every client hangs its copy on the pusher's ped (QB's bone and offsets) and the server only moves the stand's cell along (`set(item, false)` when the pusher is 8 m from the stored point). Letting go sends the client's stand position (kept only within 4 m of the ped's server position).
- Cooking: `checks` lib `minigameFor` skill checks (1 ring each, WASD) replace qb-minigames' key game; no miss = perfect, one = rare, more = common (QB's faults). Stock lives in the server shift (QB's `hotdog` item check on sale is a QB bug; no hot dog item exists in ai-inventory). Reputation is ai-core metadata `rep.hotdog` (QB's `PlayerData.metadata.rep`), level 2+ makes a lucky batch half the time (QB), capped at the level's stock.
- Selling: only to NPCs, as QB (no hot dog item a player could receive). The worker's client walks the closest free NPC it controls to the stand's front (no control requests under the lockdown); the server makes the offer (random quality in stock, 1-3 of it, price per hot dog from the level's range), checks the NPC's net id (no player, within 6 m of the stand, not served in the last 10 min, seller within 5 m) and pays into `payAccount`. A customer waits 30 s for the sale point; the seller walking more than 10 m from the stand or running out of stock stops selling.

## Contracts

- Callbacks and the server -> worker `shift` event: `resource/common/routes.ts`; the hot dog stand's: `resource/common/hotdog.ts`.
- Work vehicle: lib `spawnVehicle` (spawn type from the worker's client), plate `WORK<source>`, keys through lib `vehicleKeys` with purpose `job`, deleted when the shift ends.
- Towing: the server sends `attach` to `NetworkGetEntityOwner` of the towed car (fallback: the worker); the towed car is deleted at the depot or when the shift ends.

## Entry points

- Shift logic `resource/server/index.ts`, panel `resource/server/schema.ts` (`server/content.ts` holds the panel); depot and stop points (lib `Points`), stop route blip `resource/client/index.ts`; panel files on the client `client/content.ts`.
- Hot dog stand: server shifts `server/hotdogShifts.ts`, rent / push / cook `server/hotdog.ts`, sales `server/hotdogSales.ts`, stand stream `server/hotdogStands.ts`, panel `server/hotdogSchema.ts`; client counter and stand menu `client/hotdog.ts`, push and cooking animations `client/hotdogPush.ts`, drawn stands `client/hotdogStands.ts`, NPC customers `client/hotdogCustomers.ts`.
